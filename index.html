About

I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Chicago, where I previously received my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Computer Science. I am advised by Prof. Yuxin Chen.

My research focuses on continual and lifelong learning for foundation models and autonomous agents. I study how learning systems can acquire, transfer, and refine knowledge through ongoing interaction and accumulated experience. To address these questions, I develop and apply active learning and reinforcement learning methods. Another thread of my research explores machine learning for scientific discovery, with projects spanning PDE modeling, quantum material growth, and spectrometer design.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in discussing related research or potential collaborations!
